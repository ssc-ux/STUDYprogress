TRACE Network · An Observational Study Using Artificial Intelligence (AI) Algorithms on Electrocardiography (ECG), Point-of-care Ultrasound (POCUS), and Transthoracic Echocardiophy (TTE) to Estimate the Under-diagnosis of Transthyretin Amyloid Cardiomyopathy (ATTR-CM) Across a Diverse Range of US Health Systems.
